The health screen treats body zones with meds from the stash, food and water come from what the player eats, and the load tiers say how fast a player can move. This guide sets each one up in data and then walks through the demo's BPC_Vitals Blueprint, which drives food, water and sprinting on the raid HUD.

Body zones

The health screen (W_HealthScreen) reads the body from UNVSViewBodyZones. It lists zones in a fixed order, centre three first, then the left six, then the right six, so the screen can deal them into columns. To author your own body, make a data asset of class NVS View Body Zones and plug it in with a Screen Source Definition whose Replaces is NVS View Body Zones. The demo's are DA_PlayerBody and PDA_PlayerBodySource.

Each zone is one entry in Zones and one record in Records with the same id:

Record Id
The zone's id, such as Zone.Chest.
Hit Points, Max Hit Points
Where the zone starts and its total. The demo's chest starts at 62 of 85.
Conditions
One chip per entry, such as BANDAGED. Empty uses the record's status text, such as LIGHT BLEED, as a single chip.

A zone's colour follows its share of hit points: red at zero, the accent colour under Low Fraction (0.35), yellow under Hurt Fraction (0.75), and green above.

Blackline does not simulate wounds. A condition such as a bleed or a fracture is a line of text you author, and nothing drains hit points over time unless your game calls Hurt Body.

Treatments

Which meds treat which zone comes from UNVSViewZoneTreatments, set up the same way with a data asset and a Screen Source Definition. Each entry in Zone Fixes has a Zone Id, the Meds (Item Definitions) that treat it, and the Fixes rows shown for a zone that lists no meds. The rows say IN THE STASH or NONE IN THE STASH for each med, so the player knows what they have.

A med is an Item Definition with the Consumable type. Its Heals field is the hit points it gives back to a zone. A med with Heals at zero still treats what is wrong, as a bandage stops a bleed. The demo's field med kit heals 60.

When the player picks a zone and clicks a med in the stash, the screen checks three things: the item is a consumable, the zone needs work, and the zone lists that med. It then raises Item.Use with the zone as Subject Id. The Inventory component uses up one of the med, and the zone gets the med's hit points, loses its conditions and reads TREATED until it is whole. A zone at zero hit points needs a med whose Heals is above zero.

Heal all

The Heal all button treats every zone and fills it for cash. Its price is:

  • Heal Price Per Point (10 by default) for every missing hit point, plus
  • Treatment Price (150 by default) for every zone with something nobody has treated.

The button shows the price, and reads Not enough cash and stays disabled when the session cash is short. Pressing it raises Health.HealAll, and the body zones source spends the cash and heals. Both prices are fields on the body zones asset.

Hurt the body from Blueprint

All of these are on NVS View Screen Library:

Get Body Health
The player's health out of 100, from the hit points of every zone. Treating and healing raise it.
Hurt Body (Hit Points)
Takes hit points off the body, shared across the zones by their size. A zone never drops below 1 this way, so hunger wears the player down without blacking out a limb.

Treatment and damage last for the play session. They are not written to a save game.

Food and water

A Consumable's Food and Water fields are what it gives back, out of 100. The demo's ration pack gives 30 food and its water bottle 40 water. When the player uses one from a grid, the Inventory component removes it and adds its food and water to the session's total.

Get Nourishment returns the food and water eaten and drunk this session. Blackline keeps no food or water figure of its own, so add these to the values your game tracks. The demo's BPC_Vitals shows how.

Load tiers

The Load Tiers of your View Setup Definition name the gait each weight allows, fastest first, each with the kilograms it ends at. The demo uses SPRINT up to 20 kg, JOG to 28 and WALK to 36. The load card draws the track and shows what dropping weight would gain.

Get Carried Weight (Player)
Everything the player's pawn wears and carries, in kilograms. The stash does not count.
Get Load Tier (Player)
The tier that weight falls in, counting from 0 for the lightest. -1 is over the last tier's limit.

Blackline does not move the character. Your movement code reads the tier and sets its own speed.

The raid HUD groups

The raid HUD's navigation, vitals and crew groups start hidden. Reveal Hud Group shows or hides one:

Nav
The compass, the tracked task and the exits.
Vitals
Body, limbs, conditions and weapon.
Crew
The crew.

It returns false when no raid HUD is open. The HUD reads no keys itself, so your game decides when each group shows.

Walkthrough of the demo's BPC_Vitals

BPC_Vitals is a Blueprint Actor Component on the demo's player controller. It feeds the HUD's vitals meters, runs food and water down during a raid, and handles sprinting. It is an ordinary Blueprint graph you can open and copy.

How it is built

  1. Stand in for the built-in vitals On Begin Play it calls Set Screen Source with Replaces set to NVS View Vitals and Source set to self. From then on every screen that shows vitals reads this component.
  2. Answer Get Meters The component implements NVS View Meter Source. Its Get Meters returns five meters by id: Vitals.Health from Get Body Health, Vitals.Wind, Vitals.Food, Vitals.Water, and Vitals.Carry from Get Carried Weight against the carry limit, written as 21.2 / 36.0 KG. Health, food and water warn under half and turn critical under a quarter.
  3. Redraw on a timer A looping timer every 0.25 seconds calls Refresh Source with the player controller and self, so open screens read the new values.
  4. Run the vitals A second looping timer every 0.25 seconds runs the Live event:
    • Food and water add whatever Get Nourishment gained since the last tick, and lose their per minute rate when Is In Raid is true on the raid session.
    • Sprinting is Left Shift held while moving, with wind left, in load tier 0. It spends wind at 18 per second times one plus the load fraction. Otherwise wind comes back at 10 per second.
    • The character's Max Walk Speed is the sprint speed while sprinting, 140 over capacity, 260 from tier 2, and 420 otherwise.
    • While food or water is empty it calls Hurt Body with 0.5 hit points per second.
    • It calls Reveal Hud Group with Vitals, revealed while wind is under 100 or Left Alt is held.

To use your own game's values, keep the meter ids and wire your own figures into the meters in place of the variables.

Result

In the demo, open Health, pick the chest and click a bandage in the stash. The chest's bleed chip goes, the zone reads TREATED and the bandage stack drops by one. Press Heal all and pay the price shown. Deploy, hold Left Shift and run: the vitals appear as the wind drains, and they hide again once it is full. Pick up loot until you pass 20 kg and sprinting stops.
